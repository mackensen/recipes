## Ambassador Black

This is a non-alcoholic mixed drink based on [Shirley Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Temple_(drink)); the name is a nod to the [namesake's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Temple) adult career as a diplomat. You can substitute store-bought grenadine but it's more rewarding to make your own. The ginger beer should have a kick; something like Reed's or Maine Root.

### Grenadine

#### Ingredients

- 1 cup Pomegranate juice
- 1 cup white sugar

#### Steps

1. Add the Pomegranate juice and white sugar to a pot.
2. Heat the pot to boiling, stirring occasionally. The sugar will fully dissolve in the juice.
3. Remove from heat and let cool.

The recipe makes approximately 1 1/2 cups of grenadine. Refrigerate.

### Ambassador Black

#### Ingredients

- 1 oz grenadine
- 1 bottle of ginger beer

#### Steps

1. Pour the ginger beer into a standard drinking glass.
2. Measure out 1 oz of grenadine; a jigger is good for this.
3. Slowly pour the grenadine into the ginger beer.
